Hospital Operations & Administrative Performance Report
Sector: Healthcare Administration
Project Type: Personal Project
Tools: Microsoft Excel & Power BI
Reporting Period: January - December 2023
1. Executive Summary
This project demonstrates the application of administrative reporting practices and digital tools to organize healthcare operational data and monitor key performance indicators. It involved reviewing appointment records, maintaining patient and doctor information, monitoring departmental activities, and presenting operational insights through structured reports and an interactive dashboard.
The project aimed to support administrative monitoring, strengthen records organization, track operational activities, and provide structured performance reports that assist management in reviewing service operations and supporting administrative planning.

2. Project Objectives
•	Organize and maintain administrative records in a structured format.
•	Support accurate documentation and reporting processes.
•	Monitor appointment activity and service demand.
•	Review workload distribution across departments and medical staff.
•	Track waiting times and patient satisfaction indicators.
•	Present operational information through clear and accessible reports.
•	Utilize digital reporting tools to support administrative planning and performance reviews.

3. Data Scope
The project utilized a simulated healthcare dataset covering the period from January to December 2023.
The dataset includes:
•	100 appointment records.
•	50 patient records.
•	10 doctor records.
•	3 medical departments.
•	Appointment activity spanning from January 2 to December 29, 2023.


All records were organized, reviewed, and validated to support administrative reporting and operational performance monitoring.

5. Administrative Operations Review
5.1 Appointment Monitoring
Appointment records were reviewed to monitor service demand, measure activity levels, and track changes in appointment volume throughout the reporting period.
5.2 Department Activity Review
Operational activity was reviewed across departments to evaluate service demand and support administrative workload monitoring.
5.3 Workload Monitoring
Appointment distribution among doctors was reviewed to provide visibility into workload allocation and support administrative oversight of operational activities.
5.4 Service Performance Monitoring
Waiting time and patient satisfaction indicators were monitored to summarize service performance and identify areas that may require further administrative attention.
5.5 Monthly Activity Tracking
Appointment activity was organized and reviewed on a monthly basis to identify trends and support operational planning.
